A Complete Athletic Environment for Fitness, Training, Recreation, and Competitive Sport

The Sports & Wellness Complex is a modern, fully equipped athletic environment designed to support physical development, structured training, recreational activities, student wellness, and high-quality sports programs. Developed as a comprehensive facility, it brings together swimming pools, basketball courts, football fields, gymnastics and martial arts areas, a professional fitness and weightlifting zone, shower facilities, and dedicated activity spaces for different age groups and sections.

Created to serve both school-based programs and wider community use, the complex provides a safe, organized, and professional setting where students, athletes, coaches, trainers, and guests can participate in a wide range of physical activities. From early-years swimming to advanced fitness training, from football matches to basketball sessions, and from martial arts to strength conditioning, the facility offers a complete sports experience under one campus environment.

The complex features a large swimming pool designed for older students, advanced learners, training sessions, and structured aquatic programs. The main pool is suitable for swimming classes, training sessions, school competitions, supervised recreational use, and water-based fitness activities. Separate Toddler Swimming Pool

For younger children, the complex includes a separate smaller swimming pool designed specifically for toddlers and early-years learners. This dedicated pool provides a more age-appropriate, comfortable, and controlled environment where young children can begin developing water confidence in a safe and supportive setting.

The toddler pool allows early learners to become familiar with water through guided activities, basic movement, play-based learning, and introductory swimming sessions. Its separate design helps ensure that younger children are not placed in the same environment as older students, giving parents, teachers, and instructors greater confidence in the safety and suitability of the space.

This dedicated early-years pool is ideal for toddler swimming programs, beginner water confidence sessions, supervised play, and foundational aquatic learning.
